MyDay 1.6.1  -  release version
================================

MyDay-Desktop-Setup-1.6.1.exe   Windows 10 / 11 installer
  Double-click it and follow the steps. It installs for your user only (no admin password)
  and keeps your data when you update.

MyDay-Mobile-1.6.1.apk          Android installer (any Android phone)
  Copy it to the phone and tap it. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for the
  app you opened it from (Files, Chrome...). Installing a newer version over an older one
  keeps your data.

Pairing the two (so they stay in sync over your home Wi-Fi):
  Computer: Settings > Sync > Pair a new phone
  Phone:    Settings > Sync > scan the QR code, or type the PIN shown on the computer

Your data lives only on your own devices. Use Settings > Back up everything now and then.
